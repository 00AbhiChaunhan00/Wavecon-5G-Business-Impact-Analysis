📊 Excited to share my latest Power BI project: **Wavecon 5G Business Impact Analysis**

The objective was to analyze the business impact of Wavecon’s 5G launch and identify changes in revenue, customer activity and plan performance.

🔍 **Key Insights:**

• ARPU increased by **11.05%**, showing improved revenue per user.
• Total revenue declined slightly by **0.50%**.
• Active users decreased by **8.28%**.
• Unsubscribed users increased by **23.5%**, highlighting a customer-retention challenge.
• Plan P7 recorded a major revenue decline of **73.3%**.
• P1, P11 and P12 emerged as strong-performing plans, while P8, P9 and P10 were discontinued after the 5G launch.

💡 Based on the analysis, I recommended focusing on customer retention, promoting high-performing 5G plans and migrating P7 users to better-performing plans such as P1 and P11.
